Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Introduction

Hide n Sneak ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile game developed for entertainment purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Hide n Sneak is designed to be played offline and does not require an account, login, or any personal data to function. The game does not:

  • Collect your name, email, or contact information
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Share any data with third parties

Local Data Storage

The app stores game progress, settings, and purchase records locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is deleted if you uninstall the app

In-App Purchases

Hide n Sneak offers optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID.

For information about how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Hide n Sneak does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults. The game is suitable for players of all ages and contains no mechanisms for data collection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
